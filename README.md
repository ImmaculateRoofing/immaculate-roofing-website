# Immaculate Roofing and Construction

Custom one-page website for Immaculate Roofing and Construction in El Paso, Texas.

## Preview
Open index.html with VS Code Live Server. The site uses index.html, style.css, script.js, and images/logo.jpg.

## Before launch
- Replace demo stock roof photos with approved, licensed company/stock photos. The hero and hail section currently use online Unsplash images in style.css; these require an internet connection.
- Connect the inspection form to a real client-owned form/email service. It is intentionally preview-only right now and does not submit entries.
- Add the client's three real Google reviews in place of the clearly labeled placeholder.
- Confirm all claimed qualifications, ratings, service areas, 2005 founding date, and insurance-claim assistance language with the client.
- Review insurance-related wording against applicable Texas and New Mexico laws/requirements.
- Consider a privacy policy/disclosure once a form is connected and personal information is collected.
- Confirm client ownership of the domain, repository, and form service at handoff.
