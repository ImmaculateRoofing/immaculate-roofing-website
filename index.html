KNOW THE SIGNS

HIDDEN HAIL DAMAGE

Do Not Ignore Hidden Hail Damage To Your Roof

Not all hail damage is obvious. A hail bruise means the internal mat is fractured and granules are loosened but still in place. It is hard to see from the ground, but the waterproofing is already compromised.

Obvious granule loss is easy to spot. A bruise is not. If your roof is bruised, a small dent today becomes a leak, interior water damage, mold, and rot tomorrow.

!A bruise today equals a leak tomorrow.
